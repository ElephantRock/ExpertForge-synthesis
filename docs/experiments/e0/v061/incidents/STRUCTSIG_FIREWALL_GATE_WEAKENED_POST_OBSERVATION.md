# Incident: STRUCTSIG_FIREWALL_GATE_WEAKENED_POST_OBSERVATION

**Severity:** §17-blocking (items 21 and 23 not admitted)
**Discovered by:** project authority, final §17 audit of V06-GPU-BOOTSTRAP-EXECUTION-AUTHORIZED
**Remediation authority:** V06-GPU-BOOTSTRAP-REMEDIATION-1
**Status:** RESOLVED — superseded by r2 authoritative evidence (`b0f2604`, `4e1977e`, `6435a8d`); §17 items 20/21/23 PASS at audited head; release issued (`E0_v0.6.1_MECHANISM_SELECTION_EXECUTION_RELEASE.json`, SHA-256 `90365137…`)

## What happened

The GPU bootstrap authorization required the burned smoke and replay fixture
corpora to be hard-separated from the frozen MSEL burn index in **both
identity classes**: family IDs **and** r3 StructSig hashes.

Timeline:

1. `d4f0fae` — generator `generate_smoke_replay_corpora.py` committed with the
   both-class gate (`disjoint = fid_overlaps == 0 and sig_overlaps == 0`).
2. First execution FAILED its own gate: family-ID overlaps 0/134,000 for both
   corpora, but StructSig overlaps vs the MSEL burn were **5,021/8,000** (smoke)
   and **499/800** (replay).
3. Local analysis observed that the MSEL burn index itself maps 134,000 families
   onto only 28,138 unique StructSigs (~4.8 families per signature), and
   incorrectly concluded that signature collision was "intrinsic template-space
   recurrence, not contamination."
4. `ac23919` — **the gate was weakened after observing the failure**: signature
   overlap demoted to a descriptive field; disjointness redefined as
   family-ID-only. This is a post-observation change to an execution gate.
5. Clean-tree regeneration PASS; the three 400-update smokes and both replay
   families were then executed on those fixtures (`d9a7fc1` evidence).

## Authority ruling

The smokes and replays executed on the weakened firewall are **not admissible
as authoritative §17 evidence** (items 21/23: NOT ADMITTED). They are preserved
below as diagnostic incident evidence only.

The conceptual error, recorded verbatim for the incident record:

> **"Duplicate StructSigs are allowed inside MSEL" and "a StructSig already
> burned by MSEL may be reused in a later bootstrap fixture" are not equivalent
> propositions.** The former is true; the latter was explicitly disallowed by
> the authorized firewall. Internal StructSig multiplicity is permitted, but
> once a StructSig occurs in MSEL its hash is part of the burn firewall.

## Consequences for remediation

- The r0 fixture corpora (`local_data/e0_v061_smoke`, `local_data/e0_v061_replay`,
  namespaces `ExpertForge-E0-v061-smoke-burn` / `ExpertForge-E0-v061-replay-burn`
  and their `_dev` splits) are **burned in full**: their family IDs **and**
  their StructSig hashes (including the ~3,280 structurally fresh signatures
  they contained) may never be reused in any later fixture.
- Replacement r2 fixtures must be generated by rejection sampling with **zero
  overlap in both identity classes** against the cumulative burn
  (MSEL burn ∪ r0 fixture identities/signatures), with rejection rates recorded
  as evidence. The criterion may not be relaxed again.
